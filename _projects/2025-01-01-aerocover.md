Project Engineer | Boston University Rocket Propulsion Group | Ongoing

Designing an aerodynamic fairing for an externally-mounted downcomer on our upcoming rocket, ARGO, evaluating supersonic profiles at Mach 2.81 max-Q conditions.

- Evaluating supersonic nosecone profiles including tangent ogive and power law shapes for drag minimization
- Conducting CFD analysis in ANSYS to evaluate pressure distributions and drag coefficients across Mach numbers 1.5–3.0
- Running trajectory simulations in OpenRocket to validate aerodynamic performance across the full flight envelope
- Collaborating with team members on integration strategy and mounting interface design
