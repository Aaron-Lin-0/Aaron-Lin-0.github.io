Academic Project | Boston University | Jan 2025 – May 2025

Developed and programmed an Arduino-based room temperature monitor featuring an LCD display, TMP36 sensor, and an alert system housed in a custom ABS enclosure.

- Developed firmware in C++ for real-time temperature acquisition, LCD output, and threshold-based alerting via LEDs and buzzer
- Designed and fabricated a custom ABS enclosure using FDM additive manufacturing
- Authored a technical report evaluating system performance, identifying key limitations (battery life, component selection, manufacturing process), and proposing improvements for potential commercialization
- Full documentation available: [Technical Engineering Report](/files/temp-sensor-report.pdf)
