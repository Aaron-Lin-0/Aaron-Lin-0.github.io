## Objective

The assignment calls for a portable 2.5-DOF system that moves an end effector through at least a 2.5 × 2.5 in work area. It must fit in a backpack or be assembled by one person in less than ten minutes. Our team selected a pen plotter as the working task: two axes position the pen in the plane, and a third actuator raises and lowers it.

## Prototype

We first built one lead-screw stage to check the motor, coupler, bearing support, and carriage travel together. The next prototype crossed two stages and added a motorized pen lift. Foam-board brackets and temporary supports kept the build quick enough to expose motion and control problems before we committed to the final structure.

## Next iteration

The tests demonstrated coordinated X-Y motion and pen actuation. Positioning accuracy and repeatability remain unmeasured. The next build needs a stiffer frame, better-constrained carriages, calibrated travel per motor step, and repeated path tests across the required work area. The project is scheduled for completion in October 2026; I will add my specific contribution and measured results once they are documented.
