I am a Mechanical Engineering student at Boston University with a concentration in Aerospace Engineering, graduating in May 2028. My interests lie in propulsion systems, compressible flow, and structural analysis, with a focus on applying engineering principles to defense and space applications.

Through my work with the Boston University Rocket Propulsion Group, I have gained hands-on experience in rocket nozzle design, FEA validation, and experimental test-fire integration. I am proficient in SolidWorks, ANSYS CFD, Python, and C++, and I bring fabrication skills including FDM additive manufacturing, CNC machining, and electronics assembly.

I am seeking opportunities in aerospace engineering, particularly in defense or space sectors, where I can contribute to propulsion development and aerodynamic design.
